GST CATEGORY
Non-GST Charges not subject to GST (e.g. Property Tax, Water Charges etc.)
Exempt Qualifying maintenance / subscription / contribution. Exempt if total ≤ ₹ 7,500 subject to applicable conditions.
GST Applicable Independently taxable items (e.g. Parking Charges, Other Services etc.)
Both Qualifying items eligible for exemption ≤ ₹ 7,500; entire amount taxable if threshold exceeded.
INCLUDE IN ₹ 7,500 THRESHOLD?
Yes → This amount will be included in the monthly qualifying maintenance total for ₹ 7,500 threshold.
No → This amount will NOT be included in the ₹ 7,500 threshold calculation.
IMPORTANT NOTE If total of all "Yes" heads > ₹ 7,500, then entire qualifying amount (all "Yes" heads) will be taxable @ 18% (CGST 9% + SGST 9%). Not only the amount above ₹ 7,500.
